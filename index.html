📖 GRID BATTLE RULES

1. Actions (2 per Turn): Move (Base spd), Sprint (+2 sq), Charge (2D6, 7+ needed), Shoot, Fight (Melee), Aim (+1 Rng / reroll 1), Overwatch (interrupt, hits on 6+), Interact.
2. Combat Resolution: Attacker rolls attack dice (4+ = Hit, 6 = Critical). Defender rolls Defense dice (rolls ≥ Save cancel 1 Hit). Unsaved hits deal 1 Damage (HP loss).
3. Cover & Obstacles: Soft Cover (+1 Save vs distant fire). Walls block Line of Sight completely.
